- 2026-10-10 sim: gameplay v3 P1 behaviour — analog `ControlInput` (throttle ≥ 0.85 full / ≥ 0.5 steady / ≥ 0.15 ease / else coast; brake ≥ 0.6 hard / ≥ 0.1 soft; nothing held = coast); wheelspin (drive asking over 1.1× the grip leaves 70 % of it; climb mode has traction control); impact damage ∝ (v − v_safe)², v_safe 0.6 m/s × 1.5 with a bumper × clearance (0.8–1.3); scan zones (M1: 1, M3: 2, M7: 3 — stop under 0.1 m/s for 1.5 s, a miss adds 10 s, a centred stop adds 15 points); `SimState.scan` / `scansDone` / `scansMissed`; `Outcome.breakdown` (+ `scanPenaltyS`, `scanBonus`, `reactions`). - 2026-10-10 sim: energy tuning — drive draw × 3.5 (thrusters × 1.2); throttle levels differ in efficiency (per metre: steady ≈ 70 % of full, ease ≈ 57 %; coast and brakes draw nothing; climb mode power 1.4 → 0.7); small battery 0.5 → 0.3 Wh. - 2026-10-10 sim: weather (OVN-SIM-2, part 1) — `Mission.conditions?` / `Environment.conditions?` { windMps? (+ = headwind), gustMps?, precipitation? none|rain|heavy_rain|snow, visibility? clear|fog|night, temperatureC? }; `SimState.windMps?`, `SimState.gust?`; RunEvent `gust` { t, on, windMps }; `Observation.conditions?` (mission plan, every build) and `Observation.gusting?` (IMU only, else 'unknown'); TriggerCause `gust_start` / `gust_stop` (kind body, source imu). A mission without `conditions` is bit-identical to before (M1–M7 unchanged). Assumptions: air drag ½·ρ·CdA·v_rel² with ρ = 1.2 kg/m³ and CdA = 0.04 m² (bluff body ≈ 0.2 × 0.15 m, Cd ≈ 1.2), applied only on missions that define wind; gusts are seeded windows (one per 7 s slot, 1.5–3 s long, 60–100 % of `gustMps`), the lookahead never sees a future gust; LiPo usable capacity −1 %/°C below 20 °C, floor 50 % (`temperatureC` overrides the `cold` label's ×0.8); camera range × 0.35 in fog, × 0.25 at night, × 0.7 in snow (on top of rain × 0.6); lidar and ToF × 0.7 in fog, × 0.6 in heavy rain or snow, unaffected by darkness; ultrasonic unaffected; the scout drone is above the rain but not above fog, snow or night. Measured (heuristic, M1, seed 7): Speedster energy 26 % calm → 60 % in a 12 m/s headwind, 20 % with it behind; All-rounder on M4 finishes calm and is blown to a stop on the ice slope at 12 m/s.

- 2026-10-10 sim: gameplay v3 P2 (OVN-SIM-4) — air control and charged jump, Drive mode only (a brain's robot stays level, so M1–M9 balance is unchanged). Air: the body leaves the ground at the ground's angle (a 20° ramp = 20° nose-up) and turns at up to 60°/s: throttle nose-up (full 1, steady 0.6, ease 0.3), brake nose-down (hard 1, soft 0.5), coast holds. `SimState.pitch` is that angle while airborne.
